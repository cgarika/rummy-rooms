How to play

Goal: arrange all 13 cards into melds and declare first.

Your turn: draw one card — from the closed pile or the face-up open pile — then discard one. You can't discard the exact card you just took from the open pile.

Melds: a sequence is 3+ cards of one suit in a row (A-2-3 and Q-K-A both count). A set is 3–4 cards of the same rank in different suits.

Jokers: two printed jokers, plus every card of the wildcard rank (shown by the closed pile) stands in for anything. A wildcard used as its own rank still keeps a sequence pure.

To win: you need at least 2 sequences, and one must be pure (no jokers). The rest must be valid sets or sequences. Group your cards, select one to throw, and hit DECLARE.

Scoring: losers count the cards outside their valid melds (A and faces 10, jokers 0), capped at 80. A wrong declare is an instant 80.
